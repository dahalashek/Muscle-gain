Progressive overload

Stay mostly 1–3 reps from failure. Add reps inside the range first; when you reach the top of the range with good form, increase weight. During the first 2–3 weeks, 2 working sets per exercise is enough if soreness is high.

Calorie adjustment

Aim +0.15–0.25 kg/week. If the weekly average isn't rising after 2–3 weeks, add about 150 kcal/day. If you consistently gain more than ~0.3–0.4 kg/week, reduce slightly.
